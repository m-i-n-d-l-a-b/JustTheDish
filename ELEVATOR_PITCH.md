Recipe Summarizer — paste a recipe URL, get a clean, instant summary.

In seconds, Recipe Summarizer turns messy blog posts into a structured recipe: title, ingredients, steps, servings, and times. It validates inputs, handles errors gracefully, and retries AI calls for reliability. Share or download a simple text “PDF” right away. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Google Gemini, it’s the fastest way to save recipes without the clutter.

Ideal for home cooks who want the recipe—without the scroll.
